# Bone Marrow Donation Assessment

Haematopoietic stem cell donor evaluation assessing HLA typing, health
screening, anaesthetic fitness, and collection method suitability
(peripheral blood stem cells vs. bone marrow harvest).

## Scoring

**Instrument:** Donor Eligibility Classification with HLA Match
Grading.

- **Eligibility:** Suitable / Conditionally suitable / Unsuitable.
- **Risk level:** Low (ideal match, healthy, no comorbidities),
  Moderate (minor issues, manageable conditions), High (partial match,
  borderline fitness, significant comorbidities), Critical
  (contraindicated, serious health risks, poor match).

## What it covers

Demographics; donor registration and HLA typing; medical history;
physical examination; haematological assessment; infectious disease
screening; anaesthetic assessment; collection method assessment;
psychological readiness; and consent with eligibility decision.

## Common pitfalls

- **Skipping psychological readiness** — donation (particularly bone
  marrow harvest under anaesthesia) is a real physical undertaking;
  assessing willingness and understanding matters alongside physical
  eligibility.
- **Treating a partial HLA match as automatically disqualifying** —
  match quality is one factor among several; the eligibility
  classification weighs it against overall donor health and the
  clinical urgency of the recipient's need.
- **Not distinguishing collection methods early** — peripheral blood
  stem cell donation and bone marrow harvest have different anaesthetic
  and physical-fitness requirements; the assessment should clarify which
  method applies before finalizing fitness criteria.
