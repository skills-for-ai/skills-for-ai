# Depression Care

> ⚠️ **Ask your doctor or health care professional. This information is AI generated for education and NOT medical advice.**

Depression is a common mental health condition causing persistent low
mood, loss of interest in activities, and a range of physical and
cognitive symptoms that affect daily functioning.

## Common symptoms

Persistent sadness or emptiness, loss of interest or pleasure in
activities, changes in appetite or weight, sleep disturbance, fatigue,
difficulty concentrating, feelings of worthlessness or guilt, and
thoughts of death or suicide.

## General self-care measures

- Maintain routine, including regular sleep, meals, and activity, even
  when motivation is low.
- Stay connected with supportive people rather than isolating.
- Engage in treatment consistently (therapy and/or medication as
  prescribed).
- Break tasks into small, manageable steps when energy or motivation is
  limited.

## When to seek medical attention

- Persistent low mood or loss of interest lasting more than two weeks.
- Any thoughts of self-harm or suicide — this needs immediate attention;
  see [[urgent-emergency-care]] or a crisis line.
- Depression significantly affecting work, relationships, or self-care.

## Common pitfalls

- **Treating depression as something to "snap out of"** — it's a
  medical condition, not a character flaw or simple mood; that framing
  can add harmful shame and delay help-seeking.
- **Stopping antidepressant medication abruptly** — this can cause
  withdrawal effects and relapse; changes should be made gradually with
  medical guidance.
- **Underestimating passive suicidal thoughts** — even thoughts without
  a specific plan warrant discussion with a professional; they're not
  something to just monitor privately.
