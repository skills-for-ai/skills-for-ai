# Viral Effects

In business, "viral effects" refer to the phenomenon where a product or
service spreads rapidly through word-of-mouth or other social sharing,
similar to how a virus spreads — customers become advocates and share
their positive experiences with others, creating a self-sustaining
cycle of growth and adoption.

## How viral effects amplify

Social media platforms give customers a powerful way to share
experiences and reach a broad audience quickly. Viral effects are
amplified by an especially innovative or unique product, a strong
brand presence, influencer promotion, and a strong community around
the product that encourages sharing and creates a sense of belonging.

## The reverse risk

Viral effects can work in reverse: negative experiences spread just as
quickly as positive ones, potentially damaging a business's reputation
and growth potential. Because of this, delivering genuinely
high-quality products and excellent customer service matters directly
to managing viral risk, not just to customer satisfaction in the
abstract.

## Common pitfalls

- **Engineering "viral" features without a genuinely shareable
  experience underneath** — artificial virality mechanics (incentivized
  sharing, forced invites) tend to produce shallow, short-lived spread
  rather than the sustained cycle genuine advocacy creates.
- **Ignoring negative viral risk** — focusing purely on the upside of
  viral spread while underinvesting in product quality and customer
  service leaves a business exposed to equally fast negative spread.
- **Confusing viral effects with network effects** — a product can
  spread virally without the underlying value depending on other
  users (no network effect), and vice versa; they're different
  mechanisms that can occur together or independently.
