# Thrush Care

> ⚠️ **Ask your doctor or health care professional. This information is AI generated for education and NOT medical advice.**

Thrush is a fungal infection caused by an overgrowth of Candida yeast,
most commonly affecting the mouth (oral thrush) or genital area
(genital/vaginal thrush).

## Common symptoms

**Oral thrush:** white patches in the mouth or on the tongue, soreness,
and difficulty tasting or swallowing. **Genital thrush:** itching,
soreness, redness, and a thick, white discharge (in those with a
vagina).

## General self-care measures

- Use an antifungal treatment (cream, pessary, or oral medication) as
  directed for the affected area.
- Keep the affected area clean and dry; wear breathable, cotton
  underwear for genital thrush.
- Avoid scented soaps or products in the genital area, which can
  disrupt the natural balance and worsen symptoms.
- Manage contributing factors where relevant (blood sugar control for
  those with diabetes, since high blood sugar increases thrush risk).

## When to seek medical attention

- A first episode, for confirmation of diagnosis, since other
  conditions can cause similar symptoms.
- Frequent recurrent thrush (may need investigation for an underlying
  cause, such as diabetes).
- Thrush during pregnancy, or in an infant.
- Symptoms not improving with over-the-counter treatment.
- Oral thrush in someone with a weakened immune system.

## Common pitfalls

- **Self-treating recurring symptoms repeatedly without investigating
  why they keep recurring** — frequent thrush can be a sign of an
  underlying factor (like undiagnosed diabetes) that deserves its own
  evaluation.
- **Assuming all genital itching/discharge is thrush** — other
  conditions can present similarly; if over-the-counter thrush
  treatment doesn't help, the actual cause may be different.
- **Using antibiotics without awareness of thrush risk** — antibiotics
  can disrupt the body's natural microbial balance and trigger thrush;
  this is a known, common side effect worth being aware of.
