# Shingles Care

> ⚠️ **Ask your doctor or health care professional. This information is AI generated for education and NOT medical advice.**

Shingles is caused by reactivation of the varicella-zoster virus (the
same virus that causes chickenpox), which stays dormant in the body
after chickenpox and can reactivate later in life, causing a painful
rash.

## Common symptoms

Pain, burning, or tingling in a specific area (usually one side of the
body), followed by a blistering rash in that same area, sometimes with
fever, headache, and fatigue.

## General self-care measures

- Start antiviral medication as early as possible if prescribed — it's
  most effective when started within 72 hours of rash onset.
- Keep the rash clean and covered to reduce the risk of spreading the
  virus to others who haven't had chickenpox or the vaccine.
- Use prescribed or recommended pain relief, since shingles pain can be
  significant.
- Avoid contact with people who haven't had chickenpox or the vaccine
  (especially pregnant women, infants, and people with weakened immune
  systems) while the rash is active and blistering.

## When to seek medical attention

- Suspected shingles, ideally as soon as possible — early antiviral
  treatment reduces severity and complication risk.
- Shingles near the eye — needs prompt evaluation to protect vision.
- Pain persisting after the rash heals (postherpetic neuralgia), which
  may need specific treatment.
- Widespread rash or shingles in someone with a weakened immune system.

## Common pitfalls

- **Delaying treatment, thinking it's just a skin irritation** —
  antiviral treatment works best started early; delay reduces its
  effectiveness.
- **Assuming shingles isn't contagious** — while someone can't "catch
  shingles" from another person, the virus can spread to someone who
  hasn't had chickenpox, causing chickenpox in them (not shingles).
- **Not asking about the shingles vaccine for prevention** — a vaccine
  is available for eligible age groups to reduce the risk and severity
  of shingles.
