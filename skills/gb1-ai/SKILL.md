# GB1

GB1 is a British AI assistant from **Locai Labs Limited** (London, UK),
positioned as a privacy- and sustainability-focused alternative to Big Tech
AI assistants, with an emphasis on UK data residency and community-directed
development.

*The facts below were read from [gb1.ai](https://gb1.ai/) on 2026-09-05 —
re-check the site before treating any of it, especially pricing and
availability, as still current; this is a small, early-stage product likely
to change quickly.*

## Positioning

- **Privacy** — end-to-end encrypted conversations.
- **UK data residency** — data is stated to never leave British legal
  jurisdiction.
- **Sustainability** — the site states it's powered by 100% UK renewable
  energy and uses "significantly less computing power" than comparable
  models.
- **Community-directed development** and an emphasis on transparency,
  contrasted explicitly against large US tech providers.

## Product surface

- A chat assistant with a mobile app and a voice mode.
- Project/workspace functionality for organizing conversations.
- A named privacy feature, **"Forget-Me-Not™"**, described as a proprietary
  innovation (mechanism not detailed on the marketing page).
- Claimed strong performance on Celtic languages (Welsh, Irish, Scottish
  Gaelic) specifically, and benchmark claims against Terminal Bench 2,
  IFBench, and "BritXNLI."

## Related products (named on the site, not detailed)

- **Juno 1** — an on-premises AI appliance/station.
- **Minerva** — a training platform.
- **Locai L1 Large** — Locai's own proprietary model, presumably what GB1
  the assistant runs on.

## Access and support

No public API documentation was visible on the marketing page as of the
capture date above — GB1 reads as a consumer/team chat product first, not
(yet, publicly) an API platform. Support is via a help portal at
[help.gb1.ai](https://help.gb1.ai) and a contact form.
