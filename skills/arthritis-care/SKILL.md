# Arthritis Care

> ⚠️ **Ask your doctor or health care professional. This information is AI generated for education and NOT medical advice.**

Arthritis refers to inflammation of one or more joints, causing pain
and stiffness; the two most common forms are osteoarthritis (wear-and-
tear related) and rheumatoid arthritis (an autoimmune condition).

## Common symptoms

Joint pain, stiffness (especially in the morning or after rest),
swelling, reduced range of motion, and warmth or redness around
affected joints.

## General self-care measures

- Stay physically active with joint-friendly exercise (swimming,
  walking, gentle strength training) as advised.
- Maintain a healthy weight to reduce load on weight-bearing joints.
- Use heat or cold therapy for symptom relief as suits the individual.
- Take medication as prescribed, including for autoimmune types where
  disease-modifying treatment matters.
- Use assistive devices or joint-protection techniques where helpful.

## When to seek medical attention

- New joint pain, swelling, or stiffness that persists.
- Sudden, severe joint pain with redness and warmth (can indicate
  infection or gout and needs prompt evaluation).
- Worsening symptoms despite treatment.

## Common pitfalls

- **Avoiding exercise entirely due to joint pain** — appropriate
  activity generally helps maintain joint function and reduce
  stiffness, while complete inactivity often worsens outcomes.
- **Confusing osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis management** —
  they're different conditions (mechanical vs. autoimmune) with
  different treatment approaches; a diagnosis matters for choosing the
  right management.
- **Delaying treatment for rheumatoid arthritis** — early treatment of
  autoimmune arthritis can significantly affect long-term joint damage;
  delay has real consequences.
